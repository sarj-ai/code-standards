# SARJ300 — Config comment walls

Comment density alone penalizes licenses and careful documentation, so SARJ300
requires a deterministic wall shape: at least four comments attached to
same-indent entries, at least three weak verb-led restatements, and at least 75%
weak comments.

Directives, references, rationale, invariants, units, compatibility notes, and
upstream constraints are protected. Name jobs, steps, targets, and keys; keep
only comments that explain why or state a constraint.
