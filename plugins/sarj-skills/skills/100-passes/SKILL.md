# 100 passes

Establish scope/outcome; inspect code, deterministic controls and active PRs.
Rank improvements by benefit against complexity, risk and upkeep.

Complete 100 distinct numbered passes. Keep a private task-local ledger: target,
evidence, decision; justify no-change results. Repeated commands do not count.
Prefer deletion/existing enforcement; remove covered prose. Recheck changed
boundaries until a full review finds no material improvement. Never manufacture
changes; disclose blocked evidence.

Open small, coherent PRs for the highest-value improvements. Give each final diff
three quality reviews:

1. Correctness, contracts, meaningful validation.
2. Efficiency and resource cost.
3. Simplicity, concision, scope, duplication.

Then three material-value reviews:

1. Demonstrated problem and observable improvement.
2. Existing controls, cheaper alternatives and remaining gap.
3. Benefit after migration, complexity and upkeep.

Record findings/resolutions in PR descriptions. Improve or close your nonmaterial
PRs; repeat affected reviews and required checks after changes. Preserve scope and
authorization; merge only when authorized.

Show PR links, benefit, validation, limitations and closed proposals; claim only
completed passes and verified results.
