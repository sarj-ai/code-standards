---
name: 100-passes
description: Run a requested 100-pass improvement campaign and open only materially useful PRs.
---

Inspect scope/outcome, code, enforcement and active PRs. Rank benefit against risk,
complexity and upkeep; prefer deletion/existing controls.

Complete 100 distinct numbered reviews. Keep a private ledger of target, evidence
and decision, justifying no-change results; repeated commands do not count.
Recheck changed boundaries until a full review finds no material improvement.
Disclose blocked evidence; never manufacture changes.

Open coherent, high-value PRs. Give each final diff three quality reviews:

1. Correctness, contracts, validation.
2. Efficiency/resource cost.
3. Simplicity, concision, duplication.

Then three value reviews:

1. Demonstrated problem/improvement.
2. Existing controls/cheaper alternatives.
3. Benefit after migration/upkeep.

Record resolutions in PR descriptions. Improve/close your nonmaterial PRs; repeat
affected reviews/checks after changes. Preserve scope/authorization; merge only
when authorized. Show PR links, benefit, validation, limitations and closures;
claim only completed reviews and verified results.
